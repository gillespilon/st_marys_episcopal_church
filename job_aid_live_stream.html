Job aid—St. Mary’s Episcopal Church—live streaming

In brevi

Live streaming is the transmission of video or audio, or both, in real time or near real time to the internet. While often referred to simply as streaming, the real-time nature of live streaming differentiates it from other non live broadcast forms of streamed media such as video on demand, video logs, and video sharing platforms such as YouTube.

St. Mary’s Church Bonita Springs uses professional audio equipment, video cameras, audio mixing hardware, and various software programs to create a live stream of the Holy Eucharist in its many forms. Our videos are available on our YouTube channel.

This job aid serves to set up and be ready to live stream on our YouTube channel.

Conventions

  • blue shows text to enter
  • lightcoral shows a button or icon to press, click, or hold
    • EnterKey means “press the Enter Key”
    • ShiftKey means “hold the Shift Key”
  • red shows which item to select in a list

Turn on the equipment

  • The passcode to the equipment room is 1923.

Audio cabinet

  • The tall cabinet in the room is the sanctuary sound control system.

  • Flick up the ToggleSwitch on the outside left side of the cabinet if the system is not on.
  • Do not touch any controls inside this cabinet.

Streaming desk

  • The table in front of the window contains the PreSonus STUDIOLIVE 16.0.2 USB sound board, the monitor, headphones, keyboard, and mouse. Below the table on the left is the computer.
  • Press the Power I/O Switch, located on the back of the sound board on the right side.
  • Press the Meters InputButton under the meters at the far left to see the recording levels.
  • Press the PowerButton at the top on the front of the computer.

  • Wait about 15 s for the screen. Press the EnterKey.

  • Type the password STMsanc#1 and press the EnterKey .

Start the streaming software

  • Double click the vMix (x64) icon on the left side of the screen.
  • At the top of the screen, click the DownArrow to the right of the OpenButton and select the Preset Setup IV.vmix.
  • At the top of the screen, click the MaximizeIcon, the second icon from the right. This should make everything smaller. Click and drag the window to the left edge of the screen. Resize so that the window touches the top, left, and bottom edges of the screen.
  • You should see this:

  • Click on the GearIconfor preset 11.

  • In the resulting window, left column, click on PTZ.

  • Resize this window as appropriate. Move this window to the right so that it does not overlap with the main window and the controls are accessbile.

  • The screen should look as follows.
  • The left preview window should have an amber bar. The preset 6 window should have an amber bar. The right preview window should have a green bar. The preset 11 window should have a green bar.

Set the recording settings

  • At the bottom, click on the GearIcon next to the RecordButton.

  • In the pop-up window file name, change only the date in Filename.
  • Click the OKButton.

Set the title banner

  • Right click anywhere in the #10 preset window.
  • Click on the Title Editor Button.

  • Change the date.
  • Click the x in the upper right corner to close the window.
  • Click on the small box “1” at the bottom of the #10 preset window to turn on the overlay for the right preview window with the green bar.

Begin audio and video streaming

  • Click on the StreamButton; the button bar turns red.
  • Click on the RecordButton; it will have a red STREAM2 & REC button by top preview.
  • The FADE button is used to transition between camera 1 and camera 2.
  • The active camera is on the right with a green bar.
  • The other camera preview is on the left with an amber bar.
  • If the streaming stops for any reason, do not restart it because of how YouTube is set up. Change the file name in the record button (if it has stopped) and restart recording only, not streaming. When the service is finished and you have stopped the recording, see uploading a video manually.

Cameras

  • Camera 1 is at the left side of the main aisle, near the front, and points to the right lectern, the organist, and the choir.
  • Camera 2 is at the back of the church and points up the center aisle.

Microphones

  • Fr. Bill uses the “wireless 5” microphone (slider 13/14 on the sound board). He occasionally forgets to turn it off. Be prepared to turn down the input setting.
  • Fr. Ryan uses the “wireless 6” microphone (slider 15/16 on the sound board). He is very good about how he uses his microphone.

Downloading a church video to a computer

  • Go to the GitHub page for yt-dlp. Click on the link “yt-dlp.exe” under the “RELEASE FILES Recommend” section. This will download the file to your computer. It is safe to do so. yt-dlp is trusted, reliable, open source software.
  • Double click the downloaded file to install it.
  • Open a “command prompt” or “PowerShell” terminal. (Instructions required for this, for Windows, Mac, and Linux.)
  • Navigate to the directory that will contain the video to download.
  • Execute yt-dlp "URL". For example, yt-dlp "https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DVrs5r49TkI" where the URL was obtained from one of the church YouTube channel. (Instructions required for this, for Windows, Mac, and Linux.)

Extracting audio and video from a church video

  • Go to Download FFmpeg and download the required file. You may have to extract it. (Instructions required for this, for Windows, Mac, and Linux.)
  • FFmepg is trusted, reliable, open source software.
  • Open a “command prompt” or “PowerShell” terminal in Windows. Open a terminal in a Linux shell. (Instructions required for this, for Windows, Mac, and Linux.)
  • Navigate to the directory where your video is.

Extract audio only

  • Open the video in a browser and determine the start xx:xx:xx and stop yy:yy:yy positions to extract the audio portion.
  • For example, using the above video, ffmpeg -i 20260222_st_marys_episcopal_rite_ii.mp4 -ss 00:24:00 -to 00:37:08 -vn -c:a copy 20260222_st_marys_episcopal_carl_stewardship.m4a

Trim content

extract from mp4 video from time 00:00:00 to 00:03:24 and save as video

FILE="20251221_st_marys_episcopal_rite_ii"
ffmpeg -i ${FILE}.mp4 -ss 00:00:00 -to 00:03:24 ${FILE}_fantasia.mp4

Trim 0:00 to 5:00 from the start, 32:05 to the end, and keep everything in between.

ffmpeg -ss 00:05:00 -to 00:32:05 -i input.mp4 -c copy output.mp4

Extract from mp4 video from time 00:00:00 to 00:03:24 and save as video.

FILE="20251221_st_marys_episcopal_rite_ii"
ffmpeg -i ${FILE}.mp4 -ss 00:00:00 -to 00:03:24 ${FILE}_fantasia.mp4

ffmpeg -i ${FILE}.mp4 -ss 00:00:00 -to 00:03:24 -vn -acodec aac ${FILE}_fantasia.m4a

YouTube

St. Mary’s has a nonprofit YouTube channel. This channel is managed by Fr. Ryan Wright, Ian Mitchell, Joanna Wragg, and Gilles Pilon. Videos are automatically saved after streaming. The church website only shows a live stream video. All other videos are archived on the YouTube channel. Content on the channel is managed using studio.youtube.com.

  • Log into the church account at https://studio.youtube.com.
  • In the upper right corner of the screen you should see our church icon. Click on the icon, click on Switch account and ensure you are on the St. Mary's Episcopal Church account.
  • This should take you to the Channel dashboard.

Preparing for a live stream a day or more in advance

  • Navigate to the Channel dashboard.
  • There are three icons in the upper right corner. Click on the middle icon, the Go live icon.
  • This should take you to the Manage page. If not, click on the Manage icon on the left side.
  • click on the Schedule Stream icon on the right side of the page.
  • It will ask you if you want to Schedule with previous settings and has selected a previous service. This is the easiest way to set up a new service. Click on the Reuse settings button.
  • In the Title (required) window, change the date and other information as required.
  • In the Description window, change the information as required.
  • Click the Next button.
  • Click the Next button.
  • Change the schedule date.
  • Change the schedule time to 15 min before the service would start.

Creating a post

  • In a browser, type studio.youtube.com.
  • Ensure you are logged into the church account.
  • In the upper right corner click on Create and select Create post.
  • This will open a new tab.
  • Find the area Post an update to your fans and type a title and touch the EnterKey.
  • Add text as desired, either as one paragraph of multiple paragraphs.
  • If you want to add an image, click on the Image icon and then click on select from your computer. If you want to add more images, click on the icon below the small version of your image on the left. You can add up to ten images in this manner.
  • Click on the Post button to post it to the church account.

Uploading a video manually

  • Log into https://studio.youtube.com.
  • In the upper right corner of the screen you should see our church icon. Click on the icon, click on Switch account and ensure you are on the St. Mary's Episcopal Church account.
  • This should take you to the Channel dashboard.
  • In the top right corner, Create and Upload videos and Select files
  • Navigate to the file you wish to upload. Select it and Open
  • Title: May 17, 2026 10:00 Holy Eucharist Rite II
  • Description: The Seventh Sunday of Easter. The Sunday after Ascension Day.
  • Thumbnail: Ignore because YouTube will generate one and it’s usually acceptable. You can change this later.
  • Playlists: Select one if appropriate
  • Audience: No, it’s not made for kids
  • Age restriction: Ensure it is unchecked
  • Show more
  • Paid promotion: Ignore
  • Altered content: No
  • Collaboration: Ignore
  • Automatic chapter: Allow automatic chapters and key moments
  • Automatic concepts: Ensure it is unchecked
  • Tags: Ignore
  • Video language: English (United States)
  • Caption certification: Ignore
  • Recording date and location: Enter the date from the drop down calendar. Enter the location Bonita Springs, FL
  • License: Standard YouTube License
  • Allow embedding: Ensure it is unchecked
  • Publish to subscriptions feed and notify subscribers: Ensure is is checked
  • Shorts remixing: Ensure Don’t allow remixing is checked
  • Category: People & Blogs
  • Comments and rating:
    • Comments: On
    • Moderation: Strict
