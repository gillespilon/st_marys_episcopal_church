Thrift Shop videos

I am putting together a video for our church’s YouTube channel. Here is what I have and what I want to do. Can we do this using an ffmepg script?

Video 1

  • input1.mov recorded with an iPhone using Rode Capture and the iPhone’s built-in mic
  • mute the audio
  • trim 22 seconds from the start
  • at 00:00:28 trim to the end
  • fade in for 2 seconds at the start
  • fade out at the end to the next video for 1 second

Video 2

  • input2.mov recorded with an iPhone using Rode Capture and two wireless transmitters
  • mute both audio channels
  • use the entire video with no trimming
  • fade in for 1 second at the start
  • fade out at the end to the next video for 1 second

Video 3

  • input3.mov recorded with an iPhone using Rode Capture and two wireless transmitters
  • mute channel 2 audio
  • trim 22 seconds from the start
  • do not trim at the end
  • fade in for 1 second
  • fade out at the end to the next video for 1 second

Video 4

  • input4.mov recorded with an iPhone using Rode Capture and two wireless transmitters

  • mute channel 2 audio

  • trim 10 seconds from the start

  • do not trim at the end

  • fade in for 1 second at the start

  • fade out at the end for 2 seconds

  • video 1 is silent with a built-in silent audio track

ffmpeg -y -i input1.mov -filter_complex “[0:v]trim=start=22:end=28,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS,fade=t=in:st=0:d=2,fade=t=out:st=5:d=1,fps=30[v]; anullsrc=r=48000:cl=stereo[a]” -map “[v]” -map “[a]” -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k -ar 48000 -shortest v1_ready.mp4

  • video 2 is silent with a built-in silent audio track

ffmpeg -y -i input2.mov -filter_complex “[0:v]fps=30,fade=t=in:st=0:d=1,reverse,fade=t=in:st=0:d=1,reverse[v]; anullsrc=r=48000:cl=stereo[a]” -map “[v]” -map “[a]” -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k -ar 48000 -shortest v2_ready.mp4

  • video 3 has audio channel 2 muted

ffmpeg -y -i input3.mov -filter_complex “[0:v]trim=start=22,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS,fps=30,fade=t=in:st=0:d=1,reverse,fade=t=in:st=0:d=1,reverse[v]; [0:a]atrim=start=22,asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS,pan=stereo|c0=c0|c1=c0,afade=t=in:st=0:d=1,areverse,afade=t=in:st=0:d=1,areverse[a]” -map “[v]” -map “[a]” -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k -ar 48000 v3_ready.mp4

  • video 4 has audio channel 2 muted

ffmpeg -y -i input4.mov -filter_complex “[0:v]trim=start=10,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS,fps=30,fade=t=in:st=0:d=1,reverse,fade=t=in:st=0:d=2,reverse[v]; [0:a]atrim=start=10,asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS,pan=stereo|c0=c0|c1=c0,afade=t=in:st=0:d=1,areverse,afade=t=in:st=0:d=2,areverse[a]” -map “[v]” -map “[a]” -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k -ar 48000 v4_ready.mp4

inputs.txt:

file ‘v1_ready.mp4’ file ‘v2_ready.mp4’ file ‘v3_ready.mp4’ file ‘v4_ready.mp4’

Now run this in shell:

ffmpeg -y -f concat -safe 0 -i inputs.txt -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -preset medium -c:a aac -b:a 192k church_youtube_output.mp4
